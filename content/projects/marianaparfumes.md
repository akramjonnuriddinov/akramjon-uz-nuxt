## What I built

- **AI ordering assistant:** customers ask about perfumes in natural language. The assistant answers from the
  real product catalog (notes, prices, availability) and **places the order end to end inside the chat**.
- **Online store** with product catalog and checkout.
- **Admin panel** for products, orders, customer messages and sales statistics.
